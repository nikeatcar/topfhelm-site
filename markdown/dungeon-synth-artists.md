### Aindulmedir

**Country:** Sweden
**Genre:** Dungeon Synth
**Style:** Ambient
**Mood:** Melancholic
**Tempo:** Slow

Aindulmedir is a Swedish winter synth and dungeon synth project by Pär Boström, creating what he describes as music “for bibliophiles and hermits.” Rather than medieval battles or heroic adventures, Aindulmedir explores a quieter world of snowbound landscapes, forgotten libraries, candlelit rooms, solitude and sleep.

Its slow, dreamlike melodies blend dungeon synth with ambient and dark ambient, often sounding distant and hazy, as though heard through the walls of an ancient building during a winter storm. The music is deeply meditative and literary: an imaginary soundtrack for reading old books alone while snow falls outside. Across its albums, Aindulmedir has developed an unusually cohesive atmosphere where winter itself feels like part of the mythology.

### An Old Sad Ghost

**Country:** Austria
**Style:** Experimental
**Mood:** Dark
**Tempo:** Normal

An Old Sad Ghost is an Austrian dungeon synth project founded in 2016 by Lord Sargeburt. Rooted in the darker and more melancholic side of the genre, the project combines dungeon synth with neoclassical composition, dark ambient, lo-fi medievalism and, at times, symphonic arrangements. Its music is intensely romantic and mournful, built around themes of solitude, death, ruined places, nocturnal landscapes and emotional catharsis.

One of the defining works in the project's discography is A Letter for Carmilla, a seven-part cycle originally created between 2017 and 2018 and inspired by Sheridan Le Fanu's 19th-century Gothic vampire novella Carmilla. Across the heptalogy, Lord Sargeburt moves between minimalist piano, dark ambient passages, medievalist melodies and neoclassical dungeon synth while maintaining a persistent atmosphere of longing and melancholy. Gondolin Records later began issuing the cycle on cassette, eventually completing the series in 2026.

The project extends far beyond this cycle. Fullmoon over Videna presents bleak neoclassical dungeon synth with depressive atmosphere and moments of intense catharsis; Videna itself is an imagined hometown created by Lord Sargeburt and connected to the project's earliest material. Vierfürstenthum pushes the sound toward monumental dark symphonic dungeon synth, while Ne me mori facias develops long-form, increasingly orchestral compositions with choral elements. This unusually broad discography makes An Old Sad Ghost difficult to reduce to a single branch of dungeon synth, but melancholy, romanticism and darkness remain its central characteristics.

Rather than evoking the triumph of medieval heroes, An Old Sad Ghost often feels like what remains after their stories have ended: empty castles, forgotten tombs, dying forests and memories that refuse to disappear. It is dungeon synth shaped less by adventure than by mourning - deeply personal, theatrical and unmistakably romantic.

### Ashkahn

**Country:** USA
**Genre:** Dungeon Synth
**Style:** Percussive
**Mood:** Mystical
**Tempo:** Normal

Ashkahn is a percussive dungeon synth project by Lord Cervos, heavily inspired by The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind and the harsh landscapes of its Ashlands. Rather than relying primarily on slow synth atmospheres, Ashkahn places tribal rhythms and powerful percussion at the heart of its sound, combining them with eerie melodies, dark synthesizers and occasional samples from the game.

The result feels unusually physical for dungeon synth: rhythmic, ominous and at times almost battle-like, evoking ash storms, nomadic Ashlander tribes and the strange wilderness of Vvardenfell. The project draws directly from the world of Morrowind, while some of its melodic ideas also pay tribute to the work of Jeremy Soule.

### Ausum

**Country:** Germany
**Style:** Ambient
**Mood:** Dark
**Tempo:** Slow

Ausum is a German dungeon synth and dark ambient project built around slow-moving drones, ritualistic atmosphere and unusually tactile sound design. Rather than following the melodic medieval-fantasy tradition of dungeon synth, Ausum approaches the genre from its darker and more experimental edges, where raw dungeon synth overlaps with drone, noise and ritual ambient. The project's own Bandcamp describes its work through tags such as dark ambient, dungeon drone, dungeon noise, raw dungeon synth and ritual.

The project appeared in 2021 with Och Vath Bordak Stira, a three-track release consisting of long, slowly evolving compositions. Its sparse structure and emphasis on repetition create an inward-looking, almost ceremonial atmosphere rather than a conventional fantasy narrative. Listener An Goblin described the record as an immersive and introverted experience built from meditative drones and subtle variation - a fitting description of Ausum's early approach.

The following release, The Dark Heart of the Woods (2022), continued this language through four pieces of dark dungeon synth, drone and ambient. Ausum's physical presentation is also deliberately lo-fi: the album's cassette edition was limited to ten copies and dubbed on an old recorder specifically to introduce additional tape noise and texture into the recordings.

Ausum pushed the concept considerably further with Bone Dust (2023). Credited to S. C., the album was reportedly created using the modulated sonification of human bones. The resulting music strips dungeon synth down to a skeletal framework and combines it with dark ritual ambient and death-industrial textures: extremely slow, oppressive pieces built from drones, obscure noises and constantly shifting layers of sound. Steel Martyr later reissued the work on vinyl, describing it as a German dark ritual ambient project operating through a dungeon synth framework.

This makes Ausum an unusual entry in the dungeon synth underground. There are few heroic melodies, medieval dances or nostalgic fantasy landscapes here. Instead, the project's music is physical, claustrophobic and ritualistic - dungeon synth treated less as imaginary medieval music and more as raw material for dark sonic environments.

### Charnel Oubliette

**Country:** USA
**Style:** Ambient
**Mood:** Dark
**Tempo:** Slow

Charnel Oubliette is a Pennsylvania-based dungeon synth project devoted to death, decay and the solemn atmosphere of graveyards, mausoleums and forgotten burial places. Blending dungeon synth with funeral synth and death ambient, the project replaces heroic fantasy and adventurous medieval imagery with funereal processions, crumbling churches and meditations on mortality.

Active since at least 2018, Charnel Oubliette refers to its creator simply as “the Keeper.” Its early material already established the project's distinctive language: sparse synthesizer compositions presented as dirges, lamentations and music for the dead. Releases such as II and They Are Always with Us continued this approach, while Carving Stone for the Dead expanded it into a long-form work described as “eight prayers for the dead.”

The project's presentation is inseparable from its music. Cemetery photography, tombstones, mausoleums, deliberately archaic language and extremely limited physical editions reinforce the feeling of an obscure funerary artefact rather than a conventional album. Charnel Oubliette represents the particularly bleak and ritualistic side of dungeon synth, where fantasy gives way to mourning, mortality and the silence of the grave.

Inspired by pioneering synthesizer artists such as Mort Garson and Isao Tomita, its music combines colorful analog synths and Nintendo-like tones with pan flutes, gentle drums and lush electric pianos.

Rather than grand battles or sinister dungeons, Chestnut Brown turns small things into adventures: building a crooked cabin in the woods, exploring an unknown forest, meeting a wand maker or simply spending a quiet evening indoors. Warm, melodic and unmistakably playful, its music captures the feeling of an old childhood game whose imaginary world somehow still feels alive.

### Deep Gnome

**Country:** USA
**Style:** Comfy

Deep Gnome is a dungeon synth and comfy synth project from Maine, created by the mysterious "wizard old Toby." Its music inhabits a warm, nostalgic fantasy world of wandering gnomes, mushroom-filled forests, quiet cabins, old harbors, autumn paths, and small adventures that feel drawn from the pages of a forgotten children's storybook.

Across numerous releases, collaborations, and physical editions from labels including Out of Season, Phantom Lure, and Forbidden Keep Records, Deep Gnome has developed a remarkably consistent little universe - music for forest walks, quiet evenings with a book, and anyone who suspects that there may still be gnomes somewhere beyond the trees.

### DIM

DIM is a Canadian dungeon synth project by Josiah Wilkinson, built around vivid medieval melodies and a strong sense of storytelling. Much of its music feels like a journey through an old fantasy chronicle: wandering minstrels, peaceful valleys, ancient forests, forgotten ruins and strange lands waiting beyond the familiar roads.

### Diplodocus

**Country:** USA
**Genre:** Dino Synth
**Style:** Prehistoric
**Mood:** Epic
**Tempo:** Slow

Diplodocus is an American dungeon synth project widely regarded as the originator of Dino Synth, a peculiar offshoot of the genre devoted to dinosaurs and prehistoric worlds. Its landmark Slow and Heavy draws inspiration not only from classic dungeon synth, but from antiquated depictions of dinosaurs found in old comics, silent films and children's books.

Slow, weighty synthesizers conjure primeval swamps, lumbering herbivores and predators prowling beneath a distant Mesozoic sky. Yet beneath the ominous atmosphere lies a strong sense of wonder and nostalgia, recalling childhood dinosaur books and old documentaries as much as dark fantasy. What began as an eccentric collision of dungeon synth and paleontology ultimately helped create a tiny genre of its own.

### Elminster

**Country:** USA
**Style:** Medieval
**Mood:** Epic
**Tempo:** Normal

Elminster is an American dungeon synth project created by the prolific musician Ni'ilok and closely associated with Ithildin Tape Production. Based in Grand Rapids, Michigan, Elminster serves as one of the central projects within Ni'ilok's enormous network of dungeon synth and dark ambient aliases. While that wider catalogue explores numerous variations of fantasy ambient music, Elminster remains strongly rooted in traditional and old-school dungeon synth, combining medieval atmosphere, orchestral synthesizers, martial percussion and a pronounced sense of melancholy.

The project frequently builds its music around vast fantasy landscapes, ancient kingdoms, forests and fallen rulers. Standing at the Gates of Night (2022) is particularly representative of this approach: long-form compositions such as “Screams of the Deciduous Woods,” “The Gates of Night” and “Horns of the Old Growth” combine atmospheric passages with monumental drums and orchestral melodies. The album's official tags include medieval dungeon synth, orchestral dungeon synth and raw dungeon synth, while its imagery moves between ancient woodland, darkness and heroic fantasy.

Another important release, Kingsorrow (2022), turns toward the tragedy and grandeur of ancient rulers. Its four extended pieces - “Cappadocian Chieftains,” “Suleiman's Arch,” “Wildflowers Upon the Burial Mounds” and “The Heathen Kings of Old” - create an atmosphere in which heroic grandeur is inseparable from loss. Tape Wyrm singled out the album for precisely this quality, describing its combination of beauty and tragedy and identifying it as one of the Elminster releases closest to traditional dungeon synth. Dark Age Productions later issued the album physically and described Elminster as a prolific US dungeon synth artist taking an epic old-school journey through fallen empires.

This balance between contemplation and martial grandeur is central to Elminster's identity. The music can move from quiet, mournful synthesizer passages into heavy percussion, horns and processional melodies without abandoning its atmospheric foundation. Later live coverage by Invisible Oranges characterized Elminster's old-school approach as a form of barbarian battle-marching music, a description that captures the project's more forceful side particularly well.

Elminster is also connected to the contemporary American dungeon synth community beyond its recordings. Ni'ilok was involved in the formation of Great Lakes Dungeon Synth, a regional collective that emerged in 2023, with Elminster and Ithildin Tape Production among the scene's central projects and labels. The result is a project that feels deeply indebted to dungeon synth's past while remaining firmly embedded in its modern underground culture.

This combination of elaborate composition, folkloric influences and unabashedly adventurous spirit gives Elyvilon one of the most vibrant and immediately recognizable sounds in modern dungeon synth.

Erang is a French dungeon synth artist and one of the key figures in the genre's modern revival, creating music since 2012. His vast discography unfolds almost entirely within the Land of the Five Seasons, an imaginary realm of kingdoms, creatures and overlapping ages that transforms deeply personal memories and nostalgia into fantasy mythology.

### Ervald

**Country:** USA
**Genre:** Dungeon Synth
**Style:** Experimental
**Mood:** Mystical
**Tempo:** Slow

Ervald is an American fantasy and dungeon synth project from Cincinnati, Ohio, built around musical storytelling rather than conventional song structures. Its releases chronicle the continent of Varmiso through the eyes of Ervald, a wandering scholar-bard who records the people, places and legends encountered along his journeys.

The project's unusual compositional style is deeply personal. Ervald has severe-to-profound hearing loss, no formal musical training, and only began composing with his 2025 debut. Rather than relying heavily on repeating loops, his pieces develop as “movements and scenes,” shifting textures as the story progresses. The result often feels less like a collection of tracks and more like a fantasy novel translated into music - an unconventional approach that turns technical limitations into a distinctive form of narrative dungeon synth.

### Execration Chamber

**Country:** Canada
**Style:** Medieval
**Mood:** Dark
**Tempo:** Slow

Execration Chamber is a Canadian dungeon synth and dark ambient project created by multi-instrumentalist ZS, active since 2018. Describing its music as “arcane mysticism through spatial sonic endeavors,” the project occupies a particularly bleak and atmospheric corner of dungeon synth, combining dark ambient, modern classical composition, martial percussion and monumental synthesizer arrangements. Rather than building its identity around conventional medieval fantasy, Execration Chamber often evokes ruined civilizations, ritual spaces, death, memory and vast supernatural landscapes.

One of the project's defining early works is Cathedral Of Unbeing, recorded across 2019 and 2020. Gondolin Records described the album as apocalyptic and oppressive dungeon synth from Canada, highlighting its disturbed chords, heavy martial drums, choirs and strings. The result is an unusually massive form of dungeon synth: neoclassical and cinematic in scale, but dominated by desolation and hopelessness rather than heroic fantasy.

Execration Chamber is not limited to this martial, apocalyptic sound. Releases such as Elusion Of The Spirit explore dark ambient and modern classical territory, while later recordings increasingly experiment with improvisation and atmosphere. Dwelling, for example, was conceived as a fully improvised projection of the subconscious, dealing with lost memories, past lives and a mysterious “cold, unrelenting beyond.” This improvisational approach also appeared on the 2021 split The Unending Pale with Dutch project Vetus Supulcrum, where “To Rest Amongst The Wild” was recorded as a live improvisation.

Another side of Execration Chamber emerged with Echoes From The Wooded Shrine. Voices of the Ainur described the release as introducing Renaissance-era anthems into the project's dark dungeon music, with sophisticated arrangements and grandiose instrumentation creating a lighter and more theatrical atmosphere. Earlier material could move in the opposite direction: the label described A Presence through dense lo-fi synthesizers and black-metal-like orchestration, while The Way incorporated Middle Eastern influences.

ZS has also contributed instrumentation outside Execration Chamber. On Isegrimm's Eislandsaga, released by Gondolin in 2022, Z. Smith of Execration Chamber is credited with harp, alongside Max Berger and Medhelan's Matteo Brusa. This use of acoustic instrumentation fits the project's broader tendency to move beyond a purely keyboard-based definition of dungeon synth.

The result is a remarkably varied project held together by a recognizable sense of darkness and space. Execration Chamber can be oppressive and martial, meditative and ambient, or unexpectedly ornate and theatrical, but its music consistently feels less like a soundtrack to a conventional fantasy adventure and more like wandering through the remains of something ancient, sacred and already lost.

### Fief

**Country:** USA
**Genre:** Dungeon Synth
**Style:** Medieval
**Mood:** Adventure
**Tempo:** Normal

Fief is an American dungeon synth and medieval ambient project from Salt Lake City, Utah, and one of the most influential voices in the genre's brighter, fantasy-oriented tradition. In contrast to the darkness and isolation associated with early dungeon synth, Fief conjures an idyllic medieval world of forest paths, bustling markets, wandering bards, taverns, castles and knightly adventures.

Its intricate compositions imitate lutes, harps, recorders and other period instruments, weaving multiple melodies into richly layered arrangements inspired by medieval and Renaissance music, fantasy literature and classic RPG soundtracks. Warm, whimsical and unmistakably melodic, Fief helped establish a pastoral side of dungeon synth that feels less like exploring a forgotten crypt and more like stepping into a living medieval fantasy world.

### Tryptacobithicus

**Country:** Germany
**Style:** Prehistoric
**Mood:** Mystical
**Tempo:** Slow

Tryptacobithicus is an avant-garde Dino Synth project that drags dungeon synth millions of years into the past, replacing medieval castles, wizards and dragons with primeval jungles, prehistoric seas and colossal creatures emerging from the fog of deep time. Rather than using dinosaurs merely as an aesthetic, the project builds its releases around imaginary prehistoric beasts and their environments, creating something between dungeon synth, dark ambient, paleontology and an old monster movie soundtrack.

Each release explores its creature almost like an entry in a strange musical fossil record. The self-titled Tryptacobithicus introduces its lumbering beast through tracks named after bones, while Cocythobrachius stalks the listener through humid Cretaceous jungles as an unseen apex predator. Omegalodon carries the concept beneath the prehistoric seas. Across land, sky and ocean, Tryptacobithicus transforms the familiar fantasy logic of dungeon synth into a strange form of imaginary paleontology - music for an age when the monsters were not hiding in dungeons, but ruling the Earth.

### Ur Pale

**Country:** Poland
**Style:** Fantasy
**Mood:** Adventure
**Tempo:** Normal

Ur Pale is a Polish dungeon synth project known for its distinctive sea-themed interpretation of the genre, often described as Sea Synth. Emerging in 2017 with Sea Synth Ensemble, the project moved away from dungeon synth's traditional imagery of dark castles, forests and underground chambers toward open seas, magical islands, underwater labyrinths and fantastical voyages. Ur Pale himself explained this direction to Bandcamp Daily: he wanted his music to become more calming and to reflect the landscapes in his mind, choosing to leave the dark caves behind for the endless oceans.

Musically, Ur Pale combines melodic dungeon synth with neoclassical composition, new age atmosphere and orchestral arrangements. The project's early material is bright, flowing and unusually uplifting for the genre, with waltz-like rhythms and sweeping melodies creating the feeling of sailing through an imaginary fantasy world. Dark Age Productions described the music as “orchestral, waltz driven, melodic dungeon synth,” emphasizing the sense of hope, yearning and adventure at the heart of Ur Pale's sound.

The nautical concept runs throughout releases such as Sea Synth Ensemble, Water Tombs & Crimson Horizons and Crystal Waves Orchestra. Their titles evoke cursed sailors, emerald waves, magical piracy, submerged kingdoms, sea sorcery and mysterious voyages, turning the ocean itself into Ur Pale's equivalent of the traditional dungeon. Crystal Waves Orchestra pushed the orchestral and symphonic side further, while retaining the project's characteristic aquatic fantasy atmosphere.

With New Dawn in 2021, Ur Pale expanded this palette into an even warmer and more reflective sound. Described by the artist as a journey through the previous two years of his life, the album preserves the imagery of ultramarine waves and drifting voyages while giving the music a more personal and nostalgic character. Across the project's catalogue, Ur Pale demonstrates how far dungeon synth can travel from its darker origins while still retaining its emphasis on fantasy, atmosphere and imaginary worlds.

### Ziggurath

**Country:** Germany
**Style:** Percussive
**Mood:** Mystical
**Tempo:** Slow

Ziggurath is a German dungeon synth project by Mohammed Abdelkazeem, a musician and sound engineer whose background in early MS-DOS games, tracker music and the German extreme metal scene feeds into a deliberately nostalgic MIDI-driven sound. Rather than remaining in a single fantasy setting, Ziggurath treats each album like a journey into a different imaginary world.

Tales from Southern Realms explores desert synth through caravans, efreets, grand viziers and ancient ziggurats, while Jungle Majesty trades the dunes for tropical temples, monsoons, deadly traps and the adventures of the Tortoise King. Inspired by classic video game soundtracks, its playful MIDI arrangements turn dungeon synth into the soundtrack of a forgotten adventure game that never existed.

True North travels in the opposite direction, into frozen boreal landscapes and dark ambient. Its unusual sound was inspired by Ziggurath's battered copy of Bo Hansson's Lord of the Rings, whose melodies could barely be heard through the noise of the damaged record. Ziggurath attempted to recreate that sensation - distant music appearing like silhouettes through a snowstorm. Across desert synth, tropical synth and winter synth, Ziggurath demonstrates how far dungeon synth can travel without losing its identity.

### Örnatorpet

**Country:** Sweden
**Genre:** Dungeon Synth
**Style:** Fantasy
**Mood:** Epic
**Tempo:** Normal

Örnatorpet is a Swedish Dungeon Synth and ambient project rooted deeply in the landscapes, winters and folklore of Scandinavia. Created by an anonymous musician who began making music only after discovering the modern Dungeon Synth underground in 2017, the project transforms personal experiences of walking through Swedish forests into quiet, dreamlike musical landscapes.

Rather than focusing on heroic fantasy or medieval adventure, Örnatorpet finds magic in nature itself. Minimal synthesizer melodies, soft ambient textures and deliberate repetition evoke snow-covered forests, frozen lakes, moonlit nights and the melancholy stillness of the northern winter. Swedish folk poetry and traditional folkloric beings increasingly inhabit this landscape, allowing familiar nature to gradually become something older and stranger.

The 2019 album Hymner Från Snökulla remains one of the clearest expressions of this approach: a musical tribute to northern winters inspired partly by traditional Swedish poetry and illustrated with the artist's own photograph of the forest from which the name Örnatorpet originates. Later releases explore darker and more ethereal territory, but retain the project's characteristic restraint and connection to the natural world.

Örnatorpet's music feels less like a soundtrack to an imaginary kingdom than a walk through a real Scandinavian forest slowly becoming enchanted - a place where snow, memory and folklore begin to blur together.
